Privacy

Privacy

Short version: the Framecalm app doesn't collect anything, and this website counts visits without cookies.

The app

  • No account, no sign-in, no ads, no telemetry, no crash reporting.
  • Everything Framecalm reads (your hardware, your launchers' game lists, your games' settings files, frame-time captures) stays on your PC.
  • Its own data (backups, history, captures, settings) lives in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Framecalm. Delete that folder and it's gone.
  • Tweaks you apply are Windows settings on your PC. Framecalm keeps their old values locally so it can put them back; nothing about them leaves your PC.
  • It works offline. It makes two kinds of network request, both of which you can switch off in Settings: downloading each game's official cover image once from Steam's or the Microsoft Store's image servers (on by default; those servers see your IP address and which cover was asked for, like any website), and a check for a newer version on GitHub when the app opens (you choose at first launch, and can switch it off in Settings). Neither sends anything about you or your PC.
  • Links to graphics driver pages or Windows Settings open in your own browser or Settings app.

This website

  • Hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which doesn't use cookies, doesn't fingerprint you and doesn't track you across sites. It reports things like page views, referrers and country.
  • Fonts come from Google Fonts, the animation libraries from cdnjs and jsDelivr, and the game covers on the home page from Steam's and Microsoft's image servers, so your browser asks those servers for them.
  • The tweak demo on the home page runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you click in it is sent anywhere, and nothing on your PC is changed.
