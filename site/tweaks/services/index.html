Services tweaks

Windows services you may not need. Each one is switched to Disabled, never deleted. 19 tweaks: 9 safe, 9 advanced, 1 extreme.

  • Disable telemetry services

    Safe · Small gain. Disables the Connected User Experiences service and the WAP push service that sends diagnostic data to Microsoft.

  • Disable offline maps updates

    Safe · Small gain. Stops the service that downloads map data for the Maps app.

  • Disable Fax

    Safe · Small gain. Disables the Fax service.

  • Disable Windows Insider service

    Safe · Small gain. Disables the service used for Insider preview builds.

  • Disable store demo mode

    Safe · Small gain. Disables the service that runs the demo mode shown on PCs in shops.

  • Disable Media Player sharing

    Safe · Small gain. Disables Windows Media Player's network sharing service.

  • Disable parental controls service

    Safe · Small gain. Disables the Family Safety monitoring service.

  • Disable Payments and NFC

    Safe · Small gain. Disables the secure element service used for tap-to-pay.

  • Disable diagnostics hub collector

    Safe · Small gain. Disables a diagnostics collector used by developer tools.

  • Disable SysMain (Superfetch)

    Advanced · Fixes specific problems. SysMain preloads apps into memory based on what you use. On an SSD the gain is small, and on some PCs it causes bursts of disk use.

  • Disable Windows Search indexing

    Advanced · Noticeable. Stops the indexer reading your drives in the background.

  • Disable error reporting

    Advanced · Small gain. Crashes are no longer collected and uploaded in the background.

  • Disable Xbox services

    Advanced · Small gain. Disables Xbox Live sign-in, cloud saves and accessory services.

  • Disable location service

    Advanced · Small gain. Disables the service apps use to find where you are.

  • Disable compatibility assistant

    Advanced · Small gain. Stops Windows watching programs for compatibility problems and offering to re-run them with fixes.

  • Disable mobile hotspot

    Advanced · Small gain. Disables the service that shares this PC's connection as a Wi-Fi hotspot.

  • Disable diagnostic policy service

    Advanced · Small gain. Disables the service behind Windows troubleshooters and problem detection.

  • Disable smart cards

    Advanced · Small gain. Disables smart card readers.

  • Disable printing

    Extreme · Small gain. Disables the Print Spooler. Fewer services running, and it closes a part of Windows that has had serious security bugs.
