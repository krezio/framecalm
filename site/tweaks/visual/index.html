Look and feel tweaks

Animations and effects that make the desktop feel slower than it is. 8 tweaks: 6 safe, 2 advanced, 0 extreme.

  • Turn off transparency

    Safe · Small gain. Removes the blur behind the taskbar, Start and Settings. Frees a little GPU time on integrated graphics.

  • Turn off window animations

    Safe · Small gain. Windows open, close and minimize instantly instead of animating.

  • Open menus instantly

    Safe · Small gain. Removes the 400 ms pause before submenus open.

  • Turn off Peek

    Safe · Small gain. Hovering the corner of the taskbar no longer fades every window to show the desktop.

  • No desktop icon shadows

    Safe · Small gain. Removes the drop shadow under desktop icon labels.

  • No recommendations in Start

    Safe · Small gain. Hides recommended files and apps in the Start menu.

  • Drag windows as an outline

    Advanced · Small gain. Shows just the window frame while you drag it, instead of redrawing the whole window.

  • Icons instead of thumbnails

    Advanced · Small gain. File Explorer shows file icons instead of generating thumbnails, which speeds up big photo and video folders.
