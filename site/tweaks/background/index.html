Background apps tweaks

Apps and features that run when you didn't ask them to. 10 tweaks: 8 safe, 1 advanced, 1 extreme.

  • Stop apps running in the background

    Safe · Noticeable. Store apps such as Weather, Phone Link and Mail stop running when they're closed.

  • Stop Microsoft Edge running in the background

    Safe · Noticeable. Edge preloads itself at sign-in and keeps running after you close it. This stops both.

  • Turn off Widgets

    Safe · Noticeable. Removes the Widgets board and the news feed process that keeps it filled.

  • Turn off Copilot in Windows

    Safe · Small gain. Removes the Copilot sidebar and its background process.

  • No startup delay

    Safe · Small gain. Windows waits about ten seconds after sign-in before starting your startup apps. This removes the wait.

  • Turn off Recall

    Safe · Noticeable. Stops Windows taking snapshots of your screen to make your activity searchable, on PCs that support it.

  • Turn off Cortana

    Safe · Small gain. Removes Cortana and its background process on Windows versions that still include it.

  • No feedback requests

    Safe · Small gain. Windows stops asking how it's doing.

  • Turn off OneDrive

    Advanced · Noticeable. Stops OneDrive starting and syncing in the background, which can eat disk and upload bandwidth mid-game.

  • Turn off automatic maintenance

    Extreme · Noticeable. Stops Windows' nightly maintenance (disk optimization, scans, update cleanup) starting while the PC is idle, which can spill into a gaming session.
