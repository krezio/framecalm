Network tweaks

Latency and background bandwidth: what else is using your connection while you play. 9 tweaks: 2 safe, 7 advanced, 0 extreme.

  • Stop sharing updates with other PCs

    Safe · Noticeable. By default Windows can upload update files to other PCs. On a slow upload connection that shows up as ping spikes. Updates still download from Microsoft.

  • Turn off Remote Assistance

    Safe · Small gain. Stops other people being invited to view or control this PC through Remote Assistance. Fewer open doors.

  • Turn off network throttling

    Advanced · Small gain. Windows limits network processing while media plays so audio doesn't glitch. This removes the limit.

  • Turn off Nagle's algorithm

    Advanced · Fixes specific problems. Sends small packets right away instead of bundling them. Helps games that use TCP (some MMOs and older titles). Most shooters use UDP and won't change.

  • Stop Store apps updating in the background

    Advanced · Small gain. Microsoft Store apps stop downloading updates on their own while you play.

  • Keep Windows Update away from drivers

    Advanced · Fixes specific problems. Stops Windows Update replacing your graphics driver with an older or generic one.

  • Ask before downloading Windows updates

    Advanced · Noticeable. Windows tells you updates are ready instead of downloading and installing them in the middle of a session.

  • Prefer IPv4 over IPv6

    Advanced · Fixes specific problems. Some games and launchers connect faster or more reliably over IPv4. IPv6 still works when it's the only option.

  • Faster copies over your home network

    Advanced · Fixes specific problems. Turns off bandwidth throttling for file sharing, so copying from a NAS or another PC isn't held back.
