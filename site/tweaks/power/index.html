Power and CPU tweaks

How hard the processor is allowed to work, and what Windows switches off to save energy. 11 tweaks: 2 safe, 8 advanced, 1 extreme.

  • Keep USB devices awake

    Safe · Fixes specific problems. Stops Windows putting USB ports to sleep to save power. Fixes mice, headsets and controllers that drop out or lag for a moment after sitting idle.

  • Turn off Fast Startup

    Safe · Fixes specific problems. Fast Startup makes Shut down a half-hibernate, so drivers and Windows never get a clean start. Turning it off means Shut down really shuts down.

  • Keep the CPU at full speed

    Advanced · Small gain. Sets the minimum processor state to 100%, so the CPU doesn't have to ramp its clock up when a game suddenly needs it.

  • Turn off core parking

    Advanced · Small gain. Keeps every CPU core available instead of letting Windows park idle ones. Can smooth out frame times when a game's load jumps between cores.

  • Aggressive CPU boost

    Advanced · Small gain. Tells the CPU to use its turbo boost as soon as there's work, rather than waiting to see if the load sticks around.

  • Turn off PCI Express power saving

    Advanced · Small gain. Stops Windows lowering the power state of the link to your graphics card and NVMe drive between bursts of work.

  • Turn off power throttling

    Advanced · Small gain. Windows slows down apps it thinks are in the background. This stops it, which helps when a game's launcher, voice chat or capture app gets throttled.

  • Turn off hibernation

    Advanced · Fixes specific problems. Removes the hibernation file, which is often 6 to 25 GB on drive C:. Also turns off Fast Startup.

  • Keep drives spinning

    Advanced · Small gain. Stops Windows powering down drives after a few minutes idle, so a game doesn't hitch while a hard drive spins back up.

  • CPU energy preference: performance

    Advanced · Small gain. Tells the processor to favour speed over saving energy when it decides how fast to run.

  • Never let the CPU idle

    Extreme · Fixes specific problems. Keeps every core out of its sleep states. Shaves the wake-up delay some competitive players chase.
