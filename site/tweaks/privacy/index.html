Privacy tweaks

Ads, suggestions and data collection. Small speed effect, big quiet effect. 10 tweaks: 9 safe, 1 advanced, 0 extreme.

  • Turn off the advertising ID

    Safe · Small gain. Apps can no longer use your Windows advertising ID to show you personalized ads.

  • No tips, suggestions or sponsored apps

    Safe · Small gain. Stops Start suggestions, Settings promotions, tips notifications and apps that install themselves.

  • No tailored experiences

    Safe · Small gain. Microsoft stops using your diagnostic data to pick tips and offers for you.

  • No web results in Start search

    Safe · Small gain. Start search only looks at your PC, so it stops waiting on Bing and returns results faster.

  • No activity history

    Safe · Small gain. Windows stops recording which apps and files you use.

  • No ads on the lock screen

    Safe · Small gain. Turns off the rotating Spotlight pictures and the tips and promotions printed over them.

  • No app launch tracking

    Safe · Small gain. Windows stops recording which apps you open to sort Start and search.

  • Leave the improvement program

    Safe · Small gain. Opts this PC out of the Customer Experience Improvement Program.

  • No app compatibility telemetry

    Safe · Small gain. Stops the inventory collector that reports installed programs for compatibility analysis.

  • Minimum diagnostic data

    Advanced · Small gain. Sets diagnostic data to the lowest level your edition of Windows allows.
