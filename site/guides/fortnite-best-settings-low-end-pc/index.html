Fortnite best settings for a low-end PC or laptop

Updated November 2026 · 6 min read

Short answer

Switch Rendering Mode to Performance if your GPU is weak. Otherwise turn Shadows off, set Global Illumination and Reflections to Low, keep View Distance at Medium or Far, and set Frame Rate Limit to your monitor's refresh rate.

Fortnite runs on Unreal Engine 5, and its expensive features (Lumen lighting, Nanite, virtual shadow maps) are aimed at strong desktop GPUs. On a laptop or older card, a few settings decide almost everything.

1. Rendering Mode: Performance (weak GPUs)

Under Settings → Video → Rendering Mode, the Performance mode is built for low-end hardware and integrated graphics. It looks simpler but is by far the biggest jump on weak machines. It needs a game restart.

2. If you stay on DirectX 11/12: the heavy hitters

  • Shadows: Off. The most expensive setting on almost every GPU. Many competitive players run it off anyway.
  • Global Illumination: Low. Turns off Lumen's bounced lighting, a huge cost on mid-range cards.
  • Reflections: Low. Same story for Lumen reflections.
  • Effects: Low and Post Processing: Low. Helps in busy build fights.
  • Foliage: Low. Less grass to draw, and players are easier to spot.

3. Settings to be careful with

  • View Distance: keep it at Medium or Far. Lower can hide players and builds in the distance.
  • 3D Resolution: lowering it gives a lot of FPS but makes everything blurry. Try other settings first, and don't go below about 75%.
  • Textures: mostly about video memory, not FPS. Lower them if you have 4 GB of VRAM or less and see stutter.

4. Frame Rate Limit and V-Sync

Set Frame Rate Limit to your monitor's refresh rate (for example 144). A limit below your screen wastes frames; a much higher one just heats up a laptop. Turn V-Sync off unless tearing bothers you. On NVIDIA cards, turn on NVIDIA Reflex Low Latency.

Why your settings sometimes come back

Fortnite keeps a copy of your settings in Epic's cloud and can restore it, so a change made outside the game can be overwritten. Changing settings in the in-game menu is always safe.
