In one sentence

Framecalm is a free, open source Windows optimizer: one click builds a plan for your PC, or pick from 87 tweaks, each explained and undoable.

What makes it different

  • Shows its work. Every tweak lists the exact registry value or power setting it writes, on the public tweak list and in the app.
  • Everything can be undone. A restore point first, every original value saved and read back, and a History screen that puts any change back.
  • Leaves out the placebo. No RAM cleaners, no "network packs", no turning off Defender. The site explains why.
  • Hardware-aware. The one-click plan checks laptop or desktop, GPU and what's already set. Laptops skip tweaks that cost battery and heat.
  • Safe by design. The main app never runs as admin. A small helper does the elevated writes and refuses any key that isn't in the published catalog.
